GDPR Article 5:

Personal data shall be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently.

Organizations must collect only necessary personal data, protect it appropriately, and provide individuals with rights such as access, correction, and deletion.

GDPR Article 5(1)(e) - Storage limitation and retention:

Personal data should be kept in an identifiable form only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it is processed. Longer storage for public-interest archiving, scientific or historical research, or statistical purposes requires appropriate safeguards for data-subject rights.

Source: EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Article 5(1)(e): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
